## About Me

I am a **Hill Visiting Assistant Professor** in the [Department of Mathematics](https://math.rutgers.edu/) at Rutgers University, where I work with [Konstantin Mischaikow](https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~mischaik/). Before that, I completed my PhD at the Technical University of Munich (TUM) under the supervision of [Oliver Junge](https://gaioguy.userweb.mwn.de/) and [Ulrich Bauer](https://ulrich-bauer.org/).

I am broadly interested in the interplay of algebraic topology, dynamics, and data.
My main research focuses on understanding recurrent dynamics from data.
Although motivated by applications, this work leads to questions from a variety of mathematical fields (topological data analysis, computational geometry, representation theory, ergodic theory, ...).

I currently organize the [ACM seminar](https://sites.rutgers.edu/acm-seminar/) at Rutgers.
